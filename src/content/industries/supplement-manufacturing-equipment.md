Weld Creations fabricates and modifies stainless steel **supplement manufacturing equipment** for vitamin, supplement and nutraceutical producers across Southern California. We build the hoppers, chutes, frames, guards and components that keep encapsulation, tableting, blending and powder lines running, TIG welded to be cleaned quickly and completely between products.

Our gallery shows the environment we work in: capsule fillers and tablet presses in production rooms, stainless hoppers with perforated cones and clamp fittings, and V-blender installations. It's the same class of equipment supplement makers rely on every shift.

## What makes supplement production different

Supplement manufacturers run many products on the same equipment. A line might run a botanical powder in the morning, a vitamin blend after lunch, and a protein product the next day. That creates specific demands on equipment:

- **Frequent changeovers.** Every minute spent disassembling, cleaning and reassembling is lost production. Equipment has to come apart and go back together quickly.
- **Allergen and cross-contact control.** Residue from one product can't carry into the next. Crevices, hidden surfaces and hard-to-reach corners are liabilities.
- **Dust.** Fine powders get everywhere. Enclosures, guards and dust-collection connections have to fit well and clean easily.
- **Documented sanitation.** Manufacturers operate cleaning and sanitation programs under the dietary supplement cGMP regulation (21 CFR Part 111), and equipment that's easy to clean makes that program easier to execute and verify.

## Built for fast, thorough changeovers

We design and fabricate supplement equipment around changeover time and cleanability:

- **Fewer parts, fewer fasteners.** Consolidated weldments replace bolted assemblies wherever possible, leaving fewer places for powder to collect and fewer parts to track.
- **Clamp connections.** Tri-clamp and quick-release connections instead of bolted flanges on parts that are removed for cleaning.
- **No hidden surfaces.** Continuous welds, sealed hollow sections, radiused corners and no horizontal ledges in product zones.
- **Easy inspection.** Product-contact surfaces can be seen and reached, so sanitation can confirm they're clean.
- **Smooth, polished product contact.** Welds are blended and polished to the finish your specification calls for. See [sanitary welding](/services/sanitary-welding/).

## Materials and finishes

Most product-contact parts are **316L stainless steel**, which holds up to acidic, salty and botanical ingredients and to aggressive cleaning chemicals. **304 stainless** is standard for frames, guards and non-contact structure. We back purge full-penetration welds and deliver finishes to your specification, with passivation available through our partner network. For more on alloys and corrosion resistance, see [stainless steel welding](/materials/stainless-steel-welding/).
