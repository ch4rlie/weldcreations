Weld Creations is a precision TIG welding and metal fabrication shop in **Glendora**, California, located in the Gladstone Industrial Park at 2041 E Gladstone St, Unit Q. From here we serve manufacturers across the San Gabriel Valley and the wider Los Angeles region with sanitary stainless, exotic-alloy welding, custom fabrication and production runs.

## Why a local welding partner matters

Most of our customers could ship parts anywhere. They choose a local shop because it removes friction from the work:

- **Faster iterations.** Drop off a prototype in the morning and review changes in person, instead of waiting on freight.
- **Better replacement parts.** Bring the worn hopper, bracket or guard to the shop so we can measure the original instead of guessing from photos.
- **In-person first-article reviews.** For [production welding](/services/production-welding/), walk the first article with us before the run starts.
- **Local installs and fit-ups.** When equipment has to be fitted or installed on site, a nearby shop can plan around your shutdown window.

## What we do for San Gabriel Valley manufacturers

**[Precision TIG welding](/services/tig-welding/).** Stainless steel, aluminum, titanium and nickel alloys, from single parts to production runs, with AWS D17.1 certification.

**[Sanitary welding](/services/sanitary-welding/).** Crevice-free, back-purged stainless welds and hygienic fabrication for the region's pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, supplement and food producers.

**[Custom metal fabrication](/services/metal-fabrication/).** Enclosures, frames, stands, guards and weldments from your drawing, with cutting, machining and finishing through our partner network.

**[Production welding](/services/production-welding/).** Fixtured, repeatable runs with first-article approval and blanket orders for recurring parts.

**Exotic alloys.** [Hastelloy](/materials/hastelloy-welding/), [Inconel](/materials/inconel-welding/) and [titanium](/materials/titanium-welding/) welding for chemical, aerospace and high-temperature applications.
