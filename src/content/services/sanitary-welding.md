If you're sourcing a sanitary welding company, the questions that matter are simple. Will the welds pass your inspection, will the finish meet your spec, and will the documentation satisfy your quality team? That's what this page covers.

## What makes a weld sanitary

A structural weld only has to be strong. A sanitary weld also has to be *cleanable*, which changes how the joint is designed, welded and finished.

- **Full penetration with a smooth, flush root.** On tube and pipe, the inside bead must be consistent, with no lack of fusion, excessive root protrusion or concavity that can trap product.
- **No crevices.** Lap joints, stitch welds and gaps are where residue and biofilm build up. Product-contact seams are continuous, and joints are designed to be welded completely.
- **Controlled heat tint.** Stainless steel that gets too hot without shielding forms an oxide layer ("sugaring" in severe cases) that reduces corrosion resistance. We back purge with argon and control heat input to keep discoloration within the limits your spec allows.
- **Free-draining geometry.** Sloped surfaces, radiused internal corners and no horizontal ledges in the product zone, so equipment drains and cleans properly.
- **A surface finish that matches the spec.** Welds are blended and polished to the roughness your drawing calls out.

## Materials and finishes

Most sanitary work is **316L stainless steel** for product-contact parts and **304 / 304L** for frames, guards and structure. The low-carbon "L" grades resist sensitization, the loss of corrosion resistance next to a weld. For aggressive process chemistry we also weld nickel alloys such as [Hastelloy](/materials/hastelloy-welding/).

Finish requirements vary by industry and by part. Food-contact surfaces are commonly specified around 32 µin Ra or smoother. Pharmaceutical product-contact surfaces are often specified smoother still. Put the Ra value and any electropolish requirement on the drawing, and we'll quote the finishing along with the fabrication.

| Part zone | Typical material | What we control |
|---|---|---|
| Product contact (hoppers, chutes, tube) | 316L | Full-penetration welds, back purge, blend and polish to spec |
| Splash zone and enclosures | 316L or 304 | Continuous seams, cleanable geometry |
| Frames, stands, guards | 304 / 304L | Sealed tubing, no open ends, clean welds |
