# Quick Questions for Mark

Five questions, about two minutes on the phone. Anything left blank just means we use a safe default.

| # | Question | Why we ask | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is the business name and address on Google Maps written exactly "Weld Creations, 2041 E Gladstone Unit Q, Glendora, CA 91741"? | Google ranks the map listing higher when the website matches it character for character. | |
| 2 | Besides AWS D17.1, any other welding certifications or quality registrations? (For example AWS D1.1, ASME, AS9100, ISO 9001. "No" is a perfectly good answer.) | This is the one thing the site can't say without it being true, because buyers can check certifications. | |
| 3 | What's the smallest job worth your time? (A dollar amount or a quantity.) | So the quote form and the copy can steer small jobs away. | |
| 4 | OK to say you've built equipment for pharmaceutical / nutraceutical companies? Any customer names we're allowed to use? | The gallery shows tablet presses and cleanroom work, which is the strongest proof on the site. | |
| 5 | Any photos of you at work or of the shop you're happy to share? | Real photos beat stock photos with engineers. | |

## Working assumption
The site presents Weld Creations as a **full-service fabrication partner**: send us the drawing, and we deliver the finished part, handling machining, finishing and anything else through our shop and partner network. Capability claims are written broadly on that basis. Only *named certifications* wait for confirmation (question 2).
