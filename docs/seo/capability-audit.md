# Weld Creations: Capability Audit

**Nothing on the website claims a certification, capability or customer until it is marked Y here.**

The site is being rebuilt to win production and regulated-industry work: pharmaceutical, food-grade and aerospace. The buyers doing that work are engineers and quality managers who check claims. This sheet is the single source of truth for what the site can say. Answer what you can. "Don't know" and "skip" are fine answers.

How to fill it in: write the answer, then mark **OK to publish?** Y or N. N means we'll use the information to guide the content, but won't state it publicly.
